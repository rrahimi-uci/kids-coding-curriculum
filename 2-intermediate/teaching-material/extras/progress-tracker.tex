\begin{tip}[How to use it]
\textbf{Students:} at the end of each session, tick the \enquote{I can} statement if it is true,
and colour a star if you are proud of something. \textbf{Teacher:} fill in the group page
(last page) in the last two minutes of the session: \checkmark\ = got it, -- = not yet, ? = check
again next week. Use pencil: paths and levels change, and that is the point.
\end{tip}
