\section{Workbook exercises}
\begin{answer}[Exercise 7.1 Save High Scores]
\textbf{What to look for:} \texttt{"a"} (not \texttt{"w"}) in \texttt{save\_score()}; \texttt{\textbackslash n} at the end of each line;
\texttt{int(parts[1])} so the score is a number; empty lines skipped. Step 4: the first line looks like \texttt{Priya,90} (any nickname,
a multiple of 10 from 30 to 180). Accept \texttt{os.path.exists()} instead of \texttt{try}/\texttt{except} if a student knows it.
\end{answer}
\pyfile{../exercises/week07/ex-save-high-scores/solution.py}

\begin{answer}[Exercise 7.2 Word Guess]
\textbf{What to look for:} \texttt{strip().lower()} on every line and the empty-line check (the checks feed a file with capitals, spaces and a blank line).
Any new words are fine if they are one word, small letters, no spaces. A student who writes \texttt{" ".join(...)} in \texttt{show\_progress()} is also correct.
\end{answer}
\pyfile{../exercises/week07/ex-word-guess/solution.py}

\begin{answer}[Exercise 7.3 Dot Art from a File]
\textbf{Which mode in \texttt{save\_dots()}?} \texttt{"w"}, because saving a design should \emph{replace} the old version. With \texttt{"a"},
every save would add another copy of every dot. \textbf{Step 4:} any new picture; check that each line has 4 parts separated by commas and uses palette colors.
\end{answer}

\begin{answer}[Challenge: Mode detective]
Program A: \texttt{test.txt} holds only \texttt{blue} (each \texttt{"w"} rips out the page). Program B: \texttt{red} then \texttt{blue} on two lines.
Delete-all: \texttt{with open(filename, "w") as file: file.write("")}, opening with \texttt{"w"} empties the file.
\end{answer}

\begin{answer}[Creative]
Good dot art has every line in the form \texttt{x,y,size,color}, coordinates roughly between $-300$ and $300$, and palette colors.
Good word lists have one lowercase word per line. Praise planning on squared paper: that is designing data.
\end{answer}
