\begin{answer}[Station 4: Loot Shop]
Paper answers: total 47, cheapest 5, yes (47 is less than 50).\par
Bugs: (1) \texttt{return total} indented inside the loop, so the total is 12; un-indent it. (2) \texttt{cheapest()} returns \texttt{prices[0]}
instead of \texttt{smallest}. (3) \texttt{can\_buy()} prints instead of returning, so it gives back \texttt{None} and the shop always says
\enquote{cannot buy}; change \texttt{print(...)} to \texttt{return ...}.

\begin{answer}[Station 5: Paper detectives]
\begin{tabular}{@{}llll@{}}
n: & $-5$ & $-2$ & $-8$ \\
n > best? & no & no & no \\
best: & 0 & 0 & 0 \\
\end{tabular}\par
It gives back 0, but the biggest number is $-2$. Bug: \texttt{best} starts at 0, which is bigger than every number in the list.
Fix: \texttt{best = numbers[0]} (start with the first real number). Python's built-in \texttt{max()} also works.
\end{answer}
