\kctitle{Lesson Plans}{One plan per weekly 60-minute session, plus two buffer sessions}

\begin{tip}[How to use these plans]
Read the plan once (about 10 minutes) the day before. Every session has the same five parts:
\textbf{Warm-up, Concept, Guided demo, Hands-on build, Wrap-up}. Words in the
\enquote{Teacher script} boxes can be read aloud. Buffer sessions B1 and B2 can be used
any week: after a missed session, before a holiday, or when the group needs a change of pace.
\end{tip}
