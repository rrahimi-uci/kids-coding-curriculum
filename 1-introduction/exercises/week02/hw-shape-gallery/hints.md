## Hint 1: Where to look
Look at the two question marks in the hexagon part. They are the number of repeats and the turn. The triangle and the square above it show you the pattern. Think about how many sides a hexagon has.

## Hint 2: Try this
The rule is **turn = 360 ÷ sides**. The repeat number is the same as the number of sides. Use your calculator or the **Operators** category to work out the turn.
