# Homework 3 – Pet Reactions

**Goal:** make a virtual pet that reacts to a click, a key press and the mouse.
**Time:** about 10 minutes. **Tool:** Scratch app (offline).

## Build steps

1. Open Scratch. Keep the cat as your pet, or click **Choose a Sprite** and pick any animal.
2. Build the three scripts below. Each one starts with a different event.
3. Click the green flag. Then click the pet, press **space**, and move the mouse over the pet.
4. Save as `pet-reactions`.

## The scripts in words

Pet sprite, three scripts:

1. when this sprite clicked
   - say "Purr!" for 1 seconds
   - start sound (any sound from the dropdown)
2. when space key pressed
   - next costume
3. when green flag clicked
   - forever:
     - if touching mouse-pointer? then
       - say "Pet me more!"
     - else
       - say "" (empty)

## Expected behavior

Clicking the pet makes it purr and play a sound. Pressing space changes its
costume. After the green flag, the pet says "Pet me more!" whenever the mouse
is over it, and stops talking when the mouse moves away.
