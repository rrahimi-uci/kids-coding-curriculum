## Hint 1: Where to look
Look in the **Events** category for blocks that wait for a key. Each key is like its own doorbell. You need four separate scripts, one for each arrow key.

## Hint 2: Try this
Drag out four **when ( ) key pressed** blocks and pick a different arrow in each dropdown. Under each one, snap one block from the **Motion** category. Remember that x is left and right, and y is up and down.

## Hint 3: Almost there
Use **change x by ( )** for the left and right keys, and **change y by ( )** for the up and down keys. Moving right or up needs a positive number. Moving left or down needs a negative number. Click the stage once, then try the keys.
