## Hint 1: Where to look
A star is just a shape you can draw by moving and turning. Put that drawing in your own `star` block, so you can use it many times. The Motion and Pen categories have the drawing blocks.

## Hint 2: Try this
A five-pointed star uses the same two steps five times: move, then turn. The turn is bigger than you might think. Put the `size` oval in the move block so each star can be a different size.

## Hint 3: Almost there
In the define block, use **pen down**, then **repeat ____** with **move (size) steps** and **turn right ____ degrees** inside, then **pen up**. In the flag script, use **go to random position** (Motion category) and then **star (pick random ____ to ____)**. Use **pen up** before the loop so no lines join the stars.
