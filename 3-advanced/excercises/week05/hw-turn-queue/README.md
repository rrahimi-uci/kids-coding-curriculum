# Homework 5 - Turn Queue

**Goal:** use a queue (`collections.deque`) to give every player a fair
turn: the front player plays, then goes to the back of the line.

1. Open `starter.py` in Thonny.
2. Finish `take_turn` and `leave`.
3. Press **Run**. You should see:

```
Turn order for 6 turns: ['Priya', 'Sam', 'Kai', 'Zoe', 'Priya', 'Sam']
Kai went home. Queue now: ['Sam', 'Zoe', 'Priya']
```

**Check your work:** open `check_my_work.py` and press **Run**.
