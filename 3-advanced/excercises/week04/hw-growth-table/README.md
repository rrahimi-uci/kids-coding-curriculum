# Homework 4 - Growth Table

**Goal:** build a table that shows how the number of steps GROWS when the
list gets bigger, for linear and binary search.

1. Open `starter.py` in Thonny.
2. Finish `linear_worst` and `halving_steps`.
3. Before you press **Run**, write your predictions on the homework sheet.
4. Press **Run**. You should see:

```
   items |  linear |  binary
      10 |      10 |       4
     100 |     100 |       7
    1000 |    1000 |      10
 1000000 | 1000000 |      20
```

**Check your work:** open `check_my_work.py` and press **Run**.
