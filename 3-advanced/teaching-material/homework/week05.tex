\begin{core}[Turn Queue (about 10 minutes)]
Four friends share one game. A queue makes it fair: the player at the front plays, then goes to the back of the line.
\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=2pt]
  \item Open \texttt{week05/hw-turn-queue/starter.py} in Thonny.
  \item Finish \texttt{take\_turn}: \texttt{player = queue.popleft()}, then \texttt{queue.append(player)}, then return the player.
  \item Finish \texttt{leave}: remove a player who has to go home, and return \texttt{True} (or \texttt{False} if they were not in the queue).
  \item Run it, then open \texttt{check\_my\_work.py} and press Run. Commit your work in \texttt{git-quest}.
\end{enumerate}
\textbf{Predict:} the queue is Priya, Sam, Kai, Zoe. Who plays turns 1 to 6? \rule{8cm}{0.4pt}
\end{core}
