# Homework 3 - Hero Party

**Goal:** finish a family of hero classes (inheritance) and a `Party`
that holds them (composition).

1. Open `starter.py` in Thonny. `Hero` and `Healer` are done.
2. Finish `Builder`, `Explorer` and `Party.take_turn`.
3. Press **Run**. You should see:

```
Amara heals the team.
Kenji builds a bridge.
Sofia maps the cave.
Ravi cheers the team on.
```

**Check your work:** open `check_my_work.py` and press **Run**.

**Creative:** invent your own hero class with its own power and action.
Give your heroes names from many different places and cultures.
