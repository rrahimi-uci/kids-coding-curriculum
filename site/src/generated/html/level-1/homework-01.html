CoreWalk and Talk (Scratch, about 10 minutes)

Make a sprite walk across the stage and talk, in the right order. (No Mac at home today? Do the Challenge on paper instead and build this next week.)

  1. Open the Scratch app. Click Choose a Backdrop (bottom right) and pick any place.

  2. Build this script for the cat (or any sprite you choose). glide is in Motion.

  3. Click the green flag. The sprite should start on the left, talk, glide to the middle, talk, glide to the right and say bye.

  4. Save it: File → Save to your computer, name it walk-and-talk.

Use your own nickname and your own words in the speech bubbles.
