The big idea: tell the story of one part

A museum guide doesn’t explain every brush stroke. They pick one interesting thing and tell its story. You will do the same: pick one part of your code — your function, your loop or your if — and explain it in your own words.

TipShowcase safety

Projects are shown on the screen only: nothing is uploaded, posted or emailed. Name cards and screens show nicknames only. Photos of your artwork only if you agree, with no faces or names on the screen. If you borrowed an idea, say so in your demo.
