CoreStarry Sky (Scratch, about 10 minutes)

Make your own “star” block and fill the night sky.

  1. Open Scratch and add the Pen extension. Pick a dark backdrop (for example Stars, or paint the backdrop dark blue).

  2. My Blocks → Make a Block: name it star, add an input size, click OK.

  3. Build both scripts. Drag size out of the define hat into the move block. For the pen color, click the color circle and set Color 16, Saturation 72, Brightness 94 (yellow from the color-safe paint box).

  4. Click the flag. Save as starry-sky.

Why does the star block turn 144 degrees and not 72? (Hint: count how many times the pen goes all the way round.)
