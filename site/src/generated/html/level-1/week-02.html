The big idea

Look at these two scripts. They draw the same square! The left one copies the same two blocks four times. The right one uses a loop.

=

  • repeat 4 runs the blocks inside it 4 times, then the program carries on.

  • forever runs the blocks inside it again and again until you click the red stop sign.

How much to turn?

When you walk all the way around a shape, you turn all the way around once. A whole turn is 360 degrees. So each corner turn is:

turn = 360 ÷ number of sides

The arrows show the turn at each corner. Big arrows = big turns.
