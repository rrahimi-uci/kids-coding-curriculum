The big idea: our six big ideas

Every programming language in the world uses these six ideas. You know them all!

Big idea It means… In Scratch
Sequence (week 1) Steps happen in order, one after another.
Loops (week 2) Repeat steps so you don’t copy them.
Events (week 3) A doorbell starts a script.
Conditionals (week 3) Ask a yes/no question, then choose.
Variables (week 4) A labeled box that remembers.
My Blocks (week 5) Name a part and use it again.

TipThe four detective steps

1. What did I expect? 2. What happened? 3. Read the blocks one at a time with your finger. 4. Change one thing, then test again.
