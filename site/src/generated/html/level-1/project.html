Your mission

You are an artist who paints with code. In weeks 10, 11 and 12 you will make a Python turtle program that is a little interactive art show:

  1. it says hello to the person watching (the “viewer”),

  2. it asks them one or two questions,

  3. it draws a picture or pattern that changes because of their answers,

  4. and it has a surprise, so it is a bit different every time you run it.

In week 12 you will show it to your family at the Showcase and explain one part of your code. You can work alone or with your partner. If you work in a pair, you both need to be able to explain every part.

TipAn example

Your teacher has a finished example called Night Sky Postcard. It asks “How many stars?” and “Day or night?”, then draws the sky, a moon or a sun, random stars and a row of houses. Ask to see it, then make something that is yours.

Idea menu

Pick one, mix two, or invent your own. Every idea can be an artwork or a little game.

Asks “How many fish?” and “Deep or shallow?”. Your fish(x, y, color) function swims in random places.

Asks “How many flowers?” and “Sunny or rainy?”. Rain draws lines, sun draws a sun.

Asks “How many eyes?” and “Happy or grumpy?”. The if picks the mouth shape.

Asks “How many planets?”. Planets get random sizes; a rocket() function flies past.

Asks “How many sides?”. A loop turns and repeats your shape with random colors.

Asks “How much snow: a little or a lot?”. Snowflakes are made by a snowflake() function.

Asks “How many fireworks?”. Each firework is a burst of lines in a random place.

The program secretly picks a random number of sides, draws the shape, asks “How many sides?” and the if says “Correct!” or “Try again”.

SafetyYour art, your privacy

Use a nickname on your artwork, never your real full name, address or school. Do not ask the viewer for private things either: ask about colors, numbers, moods or animals instead. At the showcase we show projects on the screen only. Nothing is uploaded.

Where things go on the screen

The turtle window is 800 steps wide and 600 steps tall. The middle is (0,0)(0, 0). Use t.penup() and t.goto(x, y) to jump to a spot without drawing.

TipSmall steps beat big leaps

Change one thing, press Run, look. Then change the next thing. If something breaks, you know it was the last thing you changed. Save often with Cmd + S.

Make your art show react while it is open. Put this before turtle.done(). Pressing the space bar adds a new star in a random place:

Key presses with onkey

def add_star():
    x = random.randint(-380, 380)
    y = random.randint(20, 280)
    t.penup()
    t.goto(x, y)
    t.dot(8, YELLOW)

screen.onkey(add_star, "space")   # no () after add_star
screen.listen()                   # start listening for keys

Click on the turtle window first, then press the key. Ideas: "c" changes the sky color, "Up" draws a firework, "r" adds rain.
