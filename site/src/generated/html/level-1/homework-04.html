CoreMagic Dice (Scratch, about 10 minutes)

Make a dice roller that remembers how many times you rolled.

  1. Open Scratch. Make two variables (Variables → Make a Variable): roll and rolls.

  2. Build the two scripts. “join” and “pick random” are in Operators. Type a space after “You rolled” so the words don’t squash together.

  3. Click the flag, then press space to roll.

  4. Save as magic-dice.

CreativeReal dice faces

Click the Costumes tab and paint 6 costumes, one for each dice face, with 1 to 6 dots (dots, not colors, show the number). Put them in order. Then add “switch costume to (roll)” to your space-key script so the dice shows the face you rolled. Credit yourself: add a comment “Dice faces drawn by” + your nickname.
