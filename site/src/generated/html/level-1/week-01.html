SafetyHow we work together

  1. Be kind. We laugh with each other, never at a mistake. Bugs are normal!

  2. Share the mouse. The driver clicks, the navigator helps. We swap when the timer rings.

  3. Point, don’t grab. To help a partner, point at the screen and explain.

  4. Nicknames only. No real full names, addresses, school names, birthdays or photos in our projects.

  5. Offline and private. Scratch stays on this Mac. No accounts, no sharing online. If something asks for your name or a password, stop and get a grown-up.

  6. Look after your body. Take stretch breaks and rest your eyes.

The big idea

A computer is like a robot. It does exactly what you tell it, in the order you tell it. It never guesses what you meant.

A list of exact steps in order is called an algorithm. When a computer follows an algorithm, we call it a program. A mistake in a program is a bug. Finding and fixing it is called debugging.

Meet Scratch

Scratch is a coding app. You make programs by snapping blocks together, like puzzle pieces.

Exercise1.1: Hello, Cat!

Make the cat say hello, walk and say bye.

  1. Events: drag “when green flag clicked” into the code area.

  2. Motion: snap “go to x: 0 y: 0” under it. That is the middle of the stage.

  3. Looks: snap “say Hello! for 2 seconds”. Type “Hi! I am” and your nickname.

  4. Motion: snap “move 10 steps”. Change 10 to 100.

  5. Sound: snap “play sound Meow until done”.

  6. Looks: snap one more “say … for 2 seconds” and type “Bye!”.

  7. Click the green flag. Save: File → Save to your computer, folder Scratch Projects.

Done when: the cat talks, walks, meows and says bye, in that order, every time you click the flag.
