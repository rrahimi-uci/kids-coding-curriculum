CoreShape Gallery (Scratch, about 10 minutes)

Draw a triangle, a square and a hexagon in a row, like pictures in a gallery.

  1. Open Scratch. Click Add Extension (bottom left) and choose Pen. Set the cat’s Size to 40.

  2. Build the script. “pen up” before each “go to” stops the cat drawing a line between the shapes.

  3. Fill in the two missing numbers for the hexagon (use the rule turn = 360 ÷ sides).

  4. Click the flag. Save as shape-gallery.

(The right-hand stack snaps under the left-hand one: it is one long script, split in two to fit the page.)
