CorePet Reactions (Scratch, about 10 minutes)

Make a virtual pet that reacts to you in three different ways.

  1. Open Scratch. Delete the cat or keep it as your pet. Or click Choose a Sprite and pick any animal.

  2. Build the three scripts below. Each one has a different doorbell (event).

  3. Click the flag. Then: click the pet, press space, and move the mouse over the pet.

  4. Save as pet-reactions.

(The sound block may have a different name for your animal: just pick any sound from the dropdown.)
