The big idea: repeat, in words

Last week your square needed 8 lines. With a loop it needs 3. Compare:

→ 
for i in range(4):
    t.forward(100)
    t.left(90)

Python has no C-shaped block. Instead it uses two clues:

  1. a colon : at the end of the for line, and

  2. 4 spaces at the start of every line inside the loop. This is called indentation.

TipThonny helps you

After a colon, press Return: Thonny pushes the next line in for you. To come back out of the loop, press Delete once.

How many times?

range(4) gives Python four numbers: 0, 1, 2, 3. Python starts counting at 0! What matters is that there are 4 of them, so the loop runs 4 times.

A loop inside a loop

A loop can hold another loop. The outer loop picks a color. The inner loop draws a whole square. Then the turtle turns a little and the outer loop goes again.

for color in PALETTE:          # 8 colors: runs 8 times
    t.color(color)
    for side in range(4):       # draws one whole square
        t.forward(100)
        t.left(90)
    t.left(45)                  # 8 x 45 = 360

The inner lines have 8 spaces: 4 for the outer loop and 4 more for the inner loop. How many lines does the turtle draw altogether? (blank to fill in)
