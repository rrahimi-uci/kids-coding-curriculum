CoreFinish and practice (about 10 minutes)

  1. If your project is missing an ingredient, add it now (or write on paper how you will add it next week). Which ingredients still need work?

  2. Practice your 2-minute demo out loud for someone at home. They are the visitor: let them answer your program’s question (or pretend, using your sketch). Use the script in your workbook (chapter 12).

  3. Ask your visitor for two stars and a wish:

ChallengeBe ready for strange answers

Visitors sometimes type surprising things. Make your program cope:

  • a number that is too big? Use an if to make it smaller and print a friendly message;

  • Night instead of night? Add .lower() after input(...).

CreativeMake your stand card

Fold a piece of paper in half to make a card that stands up. Write your project’s title, your nickname as the artist, and one sentence about your artwork. Decorate it! (No real full names, please: nicknames only.)
