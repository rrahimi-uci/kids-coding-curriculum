The big idea

A variable is a box with a label. It holds one value at a time.

  • set score to 0 empties the box and puts in 0. Use it once, at the start.

  • change score by 1 adds 1 to what is already in the box. Use it during the game.

  • pick random 1 to 6 is like rolling a die: you get a number, but you can’t know which!

To make a variable: click Variables → Make a Variable, type a name, keep “For all sprites”, click OK. A little score box appears on the stage.

Exercise4.2: Apple Catch

Catch the falling apples in a bowl!

  1. New project. Delete the cat. Add the Bowl and the Apple sprites. Choose a backdrop you like.

  2. Make a variable called score (for all sprites).

  3. Click the Bowl and build its script. “mouse x” is in Sensing.

  4. Click the Apple and build its script. “y position” is in Motion; the “<” block is in Operators.

  5. Play! Move the mouse left and right to catch apples.

Bowl Apple (pick any sound in the dropdown)
