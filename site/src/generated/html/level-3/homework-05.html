CoreTurn Queue (about 10 minutes)

Four friends share one game. A queue makes it fair: the player at the front plays, then goes to the back of the line.

  1. Open week05/hw-turn-queue/starter.py in Thonny.

  2. Finish take_turn: player = queue.popleft(), then queue.append(player), then return the player.

  3. Finish leave: remove a player who has to go home, and return True (or False if they were not in the queue).
