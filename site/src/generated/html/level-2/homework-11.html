Add a “how to play” message to your project’s screen (with write and a font size of 18 or more), or write it on a card to place next to the computer at the showcase. Include: the goal, the keys, and how the game ends (or, for an Art Studio, how to draw, save and load). Test it on a family member: can they start without asking you anything?
