TipNo computer at home this week?

That is fine. Do the paper parts (the dev log, the Challenge plan and the Creative sketch) and bring them next week. Your project files stay in your team folder; never email or upload them.

CoreFinish your own sticky notes (about 10 minutes)

  1. Open your team’s project in Thonny. Pick one sticky note from your TO DO column that is yours.

  2. Build it in small steps. Press F5 after each step. Save with Cmd+S often.

  3. If you get a red error, read the last line of the traceback and the line number it points to. Stuck for 5 minutes? Write down what you tried and stop: that is useful too.

  4. Write a short dev log (developer diary) below.

CreativeTitle screen sketch

Design the title screen for your project on squared paper: its name, a picture, and one line that explains the keys (“← → to move, space to start”). Use colors from the Okabe–Ito palette (orange, sky blue, bluish green, yellow, blue, vermillion, reddish purple) and make sure nothing depends on color alone. Use your nickname, not your real name.
