CreativeHoliday “keep coding” menu

Pick one, two, or none: holidays are for resting too. Everything works offline, with no accounts.

  • Level up: add one wish from your feedback cards to your project.

  • Art jam: make a generative art picture with a palette list, random and a function. Write down your favorite seed.

  • Secret messages: use your Caesar cipher program to send a coded note to someone at home.

  • Teach it: show a family member how to play your game, then how one function works.

  • Remix a classic: turn a past exercise (pixel art, Etch-a-sketch, word guess) into something new. Add a credit comment.

  • Unplugged: design a board game whose rules are a game loop: what happens every turn?
