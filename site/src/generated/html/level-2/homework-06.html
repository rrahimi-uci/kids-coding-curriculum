CorePixel Pet bug hunt (Thonny, 15 minutes)

Open exercises/week06/hw-bug-pixel-pet/starter.py. It is a virtual pet stored in a dictionary, and it has three bugs. The rules: feed() takes 3 from hunger (never below 0); play() adds 2 to happiness and 1 to hunger; mood() says "grumpy" if hunger is 8 or more or happiness is 2 or less, otherwise "happy".

  1. Press Run. Read the traceback bottom-up and fix bug 1.

  2. Run again and look at the printed dictionary. Is there a key that should not be there? That is bug 2.

  3. Bug 3 does not crash. Explain mood() out loud (rubber duck!) and compare it with the rules.
