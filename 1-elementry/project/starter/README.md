# My Interactive Art Show - starter

This folder is where your final project begins.

## How to start

1. Open Thonny.
2. Click **File > Open...** and open `my_art_show.py` from this folder.
3. Click **File > Save as...** and give your project its own name, for
   example `rainbow_city.py` or `fish_party.py`. Use small letters and
   underscores, no spaces. Never put your real name in the file name.
4. Press the green **Run** button. A window opens with an orange square.
   That means everything works. Now make it yours!

## Remember your plan

Keep your plan sheet (from the project brief) next to the computer.
It says:

- what your program **asks** the viewer,
- what it **draws**,
- what **your function** is called and what it draws.

## The six ingredients

Your finished program must use all six. The comments at the top of
`my_art_show.py` have a tick list.

- [ ] `input()` asks the viewer at least one question
- [ ] a variable remembers an answer or a number
- [ ] an `if` chooses what to draw from an answer
- [ ] a `for` loop repeats something
- [ ] `random` adds a surprise
- [ ] a function you made with `def`

## Milestones

- [ ] **M1 Plan** (end of week 10): plan sheet filled in and shown to your teacher.
- [ ] **M2 Core drawing works** (week 11, first half): your function draws, the main picture appears.
- [ ] **M3 Surprise and polish** (week 11, second half): questions, `if`, `random`, a title.
- [ ] **M4 Showcase** (week 12): you can run it and explain one part of your code.

## Tips

- Work in tiny steps: change one thing, press **Run**, look, then change the next thing.
- Save often (**Cmd + S**).
- Stuck for more than 5 minutes? Read the red error message out loud to your
  partner, then ask your teacher. Bugs are normal!
- Want to see an example of a finished project? Ask your teacher to show you
  the *Night Sky Postcard*.
