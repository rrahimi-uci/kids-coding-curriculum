# Homework 6 – Remix Studio

**Goal:** practise "blocks to words" for next week's Python, and level up one
of your own Scratch projects.
**Time:** about 10 minutes for Core. **Tool:** paper + Scratch app (offline).

## Build steps

**Part 1 (paper, 4 minutes):** on your homework sheet, write the Python line for
each block. Use your card sort as a dictionary:

- move 50 steps
- turn left 90 degrees
- say "Hi!"
- repeat 3
- set lives to 3

**Part 2 (Scratch, 6 minutes):**

1. Open one of your projects from weeks 1-5 (**File > Load from your computer**).
2. Add **one** improvement: a sound effect, a new backdrop, a score or lives
   variable, a key that does something, a My Block, or a message between two sprites.
3. Test it with the green flag.
4. Save it with a new name ending in `-v2` (for example `apple-catch-v2`).
   Your old version stays safe.

## The script in words (one example improvement)

Adding a "you win" message to Apple Catch, on the Apple, at the bottom of the forever loop:

- if score = 10 then
  - say "You win!" for 2 seconds
  - stop all

## Expected behavior

Your `-v2` project still does everything the old one did, plus your one new
improvement, every time you click the green flag.

## Checklist

- [ ] I wrote a Python line for every block in Part 1.
- [ ] I added one improvement to an old project.
- [ ] I tested it and it works.
- [ ] I saved it with `-v2` at the end of the name.
