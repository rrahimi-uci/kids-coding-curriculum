# Exercise 1.3 – My Sprite Show

**Goal:** plan a 5-step show, then build it in the same order.
**Tool:** Scratch app (offline). **Work in pairs.**

## Build steps

1. Start a new project: **File > New**.
2. Click **Choose a Sprite** (the cat-face button at the bottom right) and pick any sprite you like.
   You can delete the cat: right-click it in the sprite pane and choose **delete**.
3. Click **Choose a Backdrop** (bottom right, next to the sprites) and pick a place for your show.
4. On paper (workbook page), write your 5 steps, one per line.
5. Build them under **when green flag clicked**. Start with a **go to x: y:** block so the show always starts in the same place.
6. Test with the green flag. Save as `sprite-show`.

## The script in words (one example)

Your show will be different. Here is one idea for a sprite called Butterfly:

- when green flag clicked
- go to x: -150 y: 0
- say "Welcome to my garden!" for 2 seconds
- glide 2 secs to x: 100 y: 50
- next costume
- play sound (any) until done
- say "The end!" for 2 seconds

## Expected behavior

When you click the green flag, your sprite does your 5 (or more) steps in
exactly the order you planned, starting from the same place every time.

## Checklist

- [ ] I chose a sprite and a backdrop.
- [ ] I wrote my plan before building.
- [ ] My script has at least 5 blocks under **when green flag clicked**.
- [ ] The show happens in the same order as my plan.
- [ ] Speech bubbles are on screen long enough to read.
- [ ] I saved my project.
