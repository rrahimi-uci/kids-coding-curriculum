# Homework 9 - Mini Maths Quiz

**Goal:** finish a 2-question quiz that keeps score. (No turtle this time -
everything happens in the Shell.)

1. Open `starter.py` and press **Run** (F5). Answer question 1 in the Shell.
2. Read how question 1 works: `input`, then `int`, then `if ... else`, and
   `score = score + 1` when the answer is right.
3. Follow the `# TODO`s: add question 2 (`What is 9 + 15?`, the answer is 24)
   the same way.
4. At the very end, print the score, like: `You scored 2 out of 2`.
5. Test it twice: once with right answers, once with wrong answers.

**Done looks like:** right answers say `Correct!`, wrong answers say the right
answer kindly, and the last line shows the score.
