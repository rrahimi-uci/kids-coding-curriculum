\kctitle{Play-at-Home Cards}{One optional 5--10 minute card a week, played with a grown-up}

\begin{grownup}[How the cards work]
Each card is a short game to play together once during the week: many need no computer at all.
It is optional and should feel like play. Stop while it is still fun. If your child gets stuck,
do it together and talk about what you tried: that is a great result too.
\end{grownup}
