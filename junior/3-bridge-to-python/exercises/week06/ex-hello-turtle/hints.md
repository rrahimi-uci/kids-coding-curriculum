## Hint 1: Where to look
Look at the lines in the middle of the program. One line has the word `forward` in it.

## Hint 2: Try this
`t.forward(50)` makes the turtle walk 50 steps. The number in the brackets is like the white oval on a Scratch block. Change only the number.

## Hint 3: Almost there
Make the line say `t.forward(____)` with a bigger number in the blank, like 150. Then click Save and Run.
