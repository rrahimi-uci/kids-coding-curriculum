## Hint 1: Where to look
Look at the line that starts with `for`. It is the repeat. The number in `range( )` says how many times.

## Hint 2: Try this
A square has 4 sides. Each time round the repeat, the turtle draws one side. So the repeat must go round 4 times.

## Hint 3: Almost there
Make the line say `for i in range(____):` with the number of sides of a square in the blank. Keep the colon at the end.
