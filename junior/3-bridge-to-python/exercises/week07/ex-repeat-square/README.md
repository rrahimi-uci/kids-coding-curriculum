# Exercise 7.1 - Repeat 4

**Goal:** change a Python repeat to draw a square.
**Tool:** Thonny. **Work with a partner:** Driver and Helper.

## Steps

1. Click **Load**. Open `starter.py` in the `ex-repeat-square` folder.
2. Click **Run**. The turtle draws only 2 sides.
3. Find `for i in range(2):`. It is like **repeat 2** in Scratch.
4. The two lines under it are pushed in. They are inside the repeat.
5. Change `2` to `4`. Keep the colon at the end!
6. Click **Save**, then **Run**.

## What you should see

A black square with 4 equal sides. The turtle walks one side, makes a
quarter turn, and does that 4 times. It ends where it started.

## Checklist

- [ ] I found the repeat line.
- [ ] I changed 2 to 4.
- [ ] I see a closed square.

**Extra:** use the turn card. Change `4` to `3` and `90` to `120`. What shape is it now?
