# Exercise 1.2 – Square Flower

**Goal:** make a My Block called "square" and use it 6 times to draw a flower.
**Tool:** Scratch app. **Work with a partner:** Driver and Helper.

## Build steps

1. Open your Window project.
2. **My Blocks** (pink-red): click **Make a Block**. Type `square`. Click **OK**.
3. Under **define square**, snap a **repeat 4** (orange Control).
4. Put **move 60 steps** and **turn ↻ 90 degrees** (blue Motion) inside it.
5. In the flag script, drag the two repeats back to the block list to delete them.
6. Snap **repeat 6** (orange Control) under **pen down**.
7. Put **square** and **turn ↻ 60 degrees** inside it. The turn card says 60.
8. Snap **pen up** (green Pen) at the bottom. Click the flag.

## The script in words

- define square
- repeat 4
  - move 60 steps
  - turn ↻ 90 degrees

- when green flag clicked
- set size to 40 %
- point in direction 90
- erase all
- pen down
- repeat 6
  - square
  - turn ↻ 60 degrees
- pen up

## Expected behavior

Six squares meet in the middle and make a flower.
