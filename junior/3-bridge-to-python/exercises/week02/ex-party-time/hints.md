## Hint 1: Where to look
Look at your plan card. Which part are you building now, and which sprite does it belong to? Click that sprite in the sprite list under the stage before you build.

## Hint 2: Try this
You can test one script by clicking on it in the code area. The cat sends a message with "broadcast". The balloon waits for it with "when I receive". Both live in yellow Events.
