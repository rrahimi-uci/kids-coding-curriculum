## Hint 1: Where to look
Each part works on its own, so the bug is where the parts meet. Look at the yellow Events blocks in the cat and in the balloon.

## Hint 2: Try this
Read the cat's broadcast block out loud. Then read the balloon's "when I receive" block out loud. Do they say the same word? A sprite only hears the message it is waiting for.
