# Exercise 8.2 - Two-Color Flower

**Goal:** change a working flower so it is yours, and give credit.
**Tool:** Thonny. **Work with a partner:** Driver and Helper.

## Steps

1. Click **Load**. Open `starter.py` in the `ex-two-color-flower` folder.
2. Click **Run**. Six purple squares make a flower.
3. Find the second `t.color("purple")`. It says `CHANGE ME`.
4. Change that `purple` to another color, like `gold`.
5. Click **Save**, then **Run**.
6. At the top, find `Changed by:`. Type your nickname there, never your real name.

## What you should see

A flower of six squares with thick lines. The squares take turns:
purple, then your color, then purple, then your color, all the way round.

## Checklist

- [ ] My flower has two colors.
- [ ] The colors take turns.
- [ ] The top line says who made it first, and my nickname says who changed it.

**Extra:** change the first color too.
