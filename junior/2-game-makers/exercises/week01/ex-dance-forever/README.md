# Exercise 1.2 – Dance Forever

**Goal:** see the difference between repeat and forever.
**Tool:** Scratch app. **Work with a partner:** Driver and Helper.

## Build steps

**Script 1: dance 4 times**

1. Click **Choose a Sprite** (under the stage). Pick **Ballerina**.
2. Click **Events** (yellow). Drag in **when space key pressed**.
3. Click **Control** (orange). Snap on **repeat 10**. Change 10 to `4`.
4. Put **next costume** (purple Looks) inside the repeat.
5. Put **wait 1 seconds** (orange Control) inside too.
6. Click the stage. Press space. Count her moves.

**Script 2: dance forever**

7. Drag in **when green flag clicked** (yellow Events).
8. Snap on **forever** (orange Control).
9. Put **next costume** and **wait 1 seconds** inside.
10. Click the flag. Then click the red stop sign.

## The script in words

- when space key pressed
- repeat 4
  - next costume
  - wait 1 seconds

- when green flag clicked
- forever
  - next costume
  - wait 1 seconds

## Expected behavior

Space: the Ballerina dances 4 moves, then stops by herself.
Green flag: she dances until you click the red stop sign.
