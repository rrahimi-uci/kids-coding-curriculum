## Hint 1: Where to look
Look at the bottom of the forever block. Is there a bump for another block to snap on?

## Hint 2: Try this
A forever loop never finishes. Blocks that should happen once, at the start, must come before the loop. You can drag blocks into the middle of a script: wait for the gray shadow, then let go.

## Hint 3: Almost there
The order should be: green flag, then "say ____", then "start sound ____", then the forever with move and bounce inside.
