# Exercise 2.1 – Hello Cat

**Goal:** make the cat say hi, meow and move.
**Tool:** Scratch app. **Work with a partner:** Driver and Helper.

## Build steps

1. Click **Events** (yellow). Drag in **when green flag clicked**.
2. Click **Looks** (purple). Snap on **say Hello! for 2 seconds**. Type `Hi!`.
3. Click **Sound** (pink). Snap on **play sound Meow until done**.
4. Click **Motion** (blue). Snap on **move 10 steps**. Change 10 to `50`.
5. Click the green flag.

Wait for the gray shadow before you let go of a block.

## The script in words

- when green flag clicked
- say Hi! for 2 seconds
- play sound Meow until done
- move 50 steps

## Expected behavior

The cat says "Hi!", meows, then moves a little. Click the flag again and it moves
further. Drag the cat back with the mouse.

## Checklist

- [ ] My script starts with the green flag block.
- [ ] All my blocks are snapped together.
- [ ] The cat talks, meows and moves.
