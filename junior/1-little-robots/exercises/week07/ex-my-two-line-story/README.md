# Exercise 7.3 – My Two-Line Story

**Goal:** plan a tiny story for two sprites, then build it.
**Tool:** Scratch app and your workbook. **Work with a partner:** one sprite each.

## Build steps

1. **Plan:** in your workbook, draw who talks first and who answers. A grown-up can write the words.
2. Click **File**, then **New**. Add a second sprite with **Choose a Sprite**. Try Ballerina, Ghost or Star.
3. Click **Choose a Backdrop**. Pick a backdrop you like.
4. Click the first sprite. Build: **when green flag clicked** (yellow Events), then a **say** block (purple Looks).
5. Swap Driver. Click the second sprite. Build: **when green flag clicked**, **wait 2 seconds** (orange Control), then a **say** block.
6. Click the green flag. Does it match your plan?

## The script in words

First sprite:

- when green flag clicked
- say (line 1) for 2 seconds

Second sprite:

- when green flag clicked
- wait 2 seconds
- say (line 2) for 2 seconds

## Expected behavior

The first sprite says line 1. Then the second sprite says line 2.
At the start of your show, say who made the sprites: the Scratch team.

## Checklist

- [ ] My story matches my plan.
- [ ] The second sprite waits for its turn.
- [ ] I said who made the sprites and backdrop.
