# Exercise 6.2 – Keyboard Cat

**Goal:** make the cat walk and spin with the keyboard.
**Tool:** Scratch app. **Work with a partner:** Driver and Helper.

## Build steps

**Script 1: walk**

1. Click **Events** (yellow). Drag in **when space key pressed**.
2. Click the little arrow. Pick **right arrow**.
3. Click **Motion** (blue). Snap on **move 10 steps**.
4. Click **Looks** (purple). Snap on **next costume**.

**Script 2: spin**

5. Drag in another **when space key pressed** (yellow Events). Leave it on space.
6. Click **Control** (orange). Snap on **repeat 10**. Change 10 to `24`.
7. Put **turn ↻ 15 degrees** (blue Motion) inside the repeat.
8. Click the stage once. Press the keys!

## The script in words

- when right arrow key pressed
- move 10 steps
- next costume

- when space key pressed
- repeat 24
  - turn ↻ 15 degrees

## Expected behavior

The right arrow key makes the cat walk to the right. The space bar makes
the cat spin all the way round. If the cat walks off the stage, drag it back.

## Checklist

- [ ] I have two scripts, each with a yellow key block on top.
- [ ] The right arrow makes the cat walk.
- [ ] The space bar makes the cat spin.
