# Exercise 4.2 – The Noisy Cat (bug hunt)

**Goal:** count the meows, and fix the repeat so the cat meows 3 times.
**Tool:** Scratch app. **Work with a partner:** Driver and Helper.

## Build steps

1. Click the cat in the sprite list under the stage.
2. Click **Events** (yellow). Drag in **when green flag clicked**.
3. Click **Control** (orange). Snap on **repeat 10**. Leave it at 10.
4. Click **Sound** (pink). Drag **play sound Meow until done** inside the repeat.
5. Test: click the green flag. The Helper counts the meows out loud.
6. Too noisy? Click the red stop sign.
7. Leo wants 3 meows. Fix the number. Test again.

## The script in words (with the bug)

- when green flag clicked
- repeat 10
    - play sound Meow until done

## Expected behavior

With the bug, the cat meows 10 times. That is too many! After the fix, the cat
meows 3 times and stops.

## Checklist
