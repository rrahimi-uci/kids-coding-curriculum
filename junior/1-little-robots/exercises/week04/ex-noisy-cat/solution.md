# Teacher reference: Exercise 4.2 - The Noisy Cat (bug hunt)
> TEACHER ONLY. Keep this file away from students. It is not published on the website.

**Level and week:** Junior Level 1, Week 4 (Repeat It!)  **Kind:** Scratch (offline app), bug hunt  **Time:** about 6 minutes
**Big idea:** The number in a repeat block says how many times the loop runs; too many or too few is a bug we fix by testing and counting.

## The finished project
Sprite: Sprite1 (the cat), after the fix
- when green flag clicked
- repeat 3
    - play sound Meow until done

## What you should see
- With the bug: the cat meows 10 times in a row (about 10 seconds). The red stop sign stops it early.
- After the fix: the cat meows 3 times, then stops.

## Bug-hunt answer
The bug is too many repeats: the repeat block says 10, but Leo wants 3 meows. Fix: click the 10 in the repeat block and type 3. Test again and count.

## Talking points
- Link to the dance loop with REPEAT 10 that made everyone tired: too many.
- Exercise 4.3 is the other bug: too few (repeat 2 for 4 dance moves).
- Testing means counting: the Helper counts out loud while the Driver clicks the flag.

## Common mistakes and quick fixes
- Script built on the ballerina (still selected from 4.1): the sound dropdown shows her sounds, not Meow. Click the cat in the sprite list and build it there.
- Sound block under the repeat, not inside: the cat meows only once. Drag it into the C.
- "start sound Meow" instead of "play sound Meow until done": the meows pile up into one short, choppy sound. Use "play sound Meow until done".
- Changed the number to 4 because of 4.1: count with the child, then match Leo's wish.

## Check-off list
- [ ] The child counted 10 meows and said "too many": ask "how many did you hear?"
- [ ] The repeat now says 3: read the number together.
- [ ] Three meows, then silence: listen to one run.
