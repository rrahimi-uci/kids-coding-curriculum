## Hint 1: Where to look
Is the ballerina picked in the sprite list under the stage? The blocks go on the sprite that is picked.

## Hint 2: Try this
The **repeat** block is in orange **Control**. It looks like a big C. Blocks go inside the mouth of the C.

## Hint 3: Almost there
Inside the repeat you need two blocks: one that changes her pose, and one that waits. Then change the number to how many moves she has.
