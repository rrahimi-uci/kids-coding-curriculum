# Teacher reference: Exercise 4.1 - Dance, Ballerina!
> TEACHER ONLY. Keep this file away from students. It is not published on the website.

**Level and week:** Junior Level 1, Week 4 (Repeat It!)  **Kind:** Scratch (offline app)  **Time:** about 8 minutes
**Big idea:** A repeat loop runs the blocks inside it again, so we do not snap on the same blocks many times. Changing costumes over and over is animation.

## The finished project
Setup: File > New. Add the Ballerina sprite from Choose a Sprite (the cat can stay on the stage).

Sprite: Ballerina
- when green flag clicked
- repeat 4
    - next costume
    - wait 1 seconds

## What you should see
- After the green flag: the ballerina changes pose 4 times, one pose each second (about 4 seconds in total).
- She ends in the same pose she started in, because she has exactly 4 costumes.

## Talking points
- The long way needs 8 blocks under the flag; the repeat way needs 2 inside one repeat.
- Link to the dance loops warm-up: the scarf around the cards is the C of the repeat block.
- A costume is a picture of the sprite; changing it again and again looks like moving (like a cartoon or a flip book).
- Week 3 link: without the wait, the poses flash by too fast to see.

## Common mistakes and quick fixes
- Script built on the cat: the cat flickers between its 2 costumes. Drag the script onto the Ballerina in the sprite list (it copies), then delete it from the cat.
- Blocks under the repeat, not inside: one move only, after a pause. Drag them into the mouth of the C.
- Number left at 10: she dances two and a half rounds. Count together and change it to 4.
- No wait: the costumes change too fast to see. Add "wait 1 seconds" inside the loop.

## Check-off list
- [ ] The script is on the Ballerina: she is highlighted in the sprite list.
- [ ] "next costume" and "wait 1 seconds" are inside the C: dragging the repeat moves both.
- [ ] She does 4 moves and ends in her first pose: count one run out loud.
