# Exercise 4.1 – Dance, Ballerina!

**Goal:** make the ballerina do her 4 dance moves with a repeat loop.
**Tool:** Scratch app. **Work with a partner:** Driver and Helper.

## Build steps

1. Click **Choose a Sprite** (the cat button under the stage). Pick **Ballerina**.
2. Click the **Costumes** tab. Count her dance moves. Click the **Code** tab.
3. Click **Events** (yellow). Drag in **when green flag clicked**.
4. Click **Control** (orange). Snap on **repeat 10**. Change 10 to `4`.
5. Click **Looks** (purple). Drag **next costume** inside the repeat.
6. Click **Control** (orange). Drag **wait 1 seconds** inside the repeat, under next costume.
7. Click the green flag. Count her moves out loud.

## The script in words

- when green flag clicked
- repeat 4
    - next costume
    - wait 1 seconds

## Expected behavior

The ballerina changes pose 4 times, one move every second. She ends in the pose
where she started.

## Checklist

- [ ] My script is on the ballerina.
- [ ] Next costume and wait are inside the repeat.
- [ ] She does 4 dance moves.
