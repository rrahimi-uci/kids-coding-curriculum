# Teacher reference: Exercise 8.1 - Draw a Square
> TEACHER ONLY. Keep this file away from students. It is not published on the website.

**Level and week:** Junior Level 1, Week 8 (Draw With the Pen)  **Kind:** Scratch (offline app)  **Time:** about 8 minutes
**Big idea:** With the pen down, a sprite draws where it walks; repeat 4 of move and a quarter turn (90) draws a square.

## The finished project
Setup: File > New. Keep the cat. Add Extension (bottom-left) > Pen.

Sprite: Sprite1 (the cat)
- when green flag clicked
- set size to 40 %
- erase all
- pen down
- repeat 4
  - move 100 steps
  - turn ↻ 90 degrees
- pen up

## What you should see
- After the green flag: the cat shrinks, the stage is cleared, and the cat draws a square (right, down, left, up) and ends where it started, facing the same way.
- Each new click draws the same square in the same place, because the cat comes home.
- Any size of square is fine if all four sides are drawn and the square fits on the stage.

## Talking points
- Pen down is the crayon on the paper; pen up lifts it off; erase all is the big eraser.
- A quarter turn is 90 in Scratch. Four quarter turns bring the cat back to where it started.
- The repeat saves us from building move and turn four times: link to the warm-up walk.

## Common mistakes and quick fixes
- No green Pen circle: add the Pen extension (it is per project).
- Nothing is drawn: "pen down" is missing or sits under the repeat.
- Old lines stay: "erase all" is missing at the top.
- The square is cut off: drag the cat to a place with more room, or use a smaller move.
- Lines appear when dragging the cat: the pen is still down. Click "pen up" in the Pen list, then "erase all".

## Check-off list

- [ ] Move 100 and turn 90 are both inside "repeat 4": the C-shaped block holds two blocks.
