\kctitle{My Animated Greeting Card}{Project brief, storyboard and stars (weeks 9 and 10)}

\begin{grownup}[How to use this brief]
Read it aloud together at the start of week 9. Your child draws on the storyboard page
before building. Saying, pointing and drawing all count: no writing is needed.
\end{grownup}

\section{Your mission}
Make a Scratch card for someone special.\par
It moves. It talks. It makes a sound.\par
In week 10, you show it to your family!

\begin{safety}[Nicknames and credit]
Write a first name or a nickname on your card, never a full name.
The sprites and sounds come from the Scratch library. Say so at the showcase!
\end{safety}

\section{Need a different path?}
\begin{tip}[Core path: start from a card]
A grown-up sets up the card for you. The backdrop and the first sprite are already there.
Add one must-have at a time, and click the flag after each one.
\end{tip}

\begin{stretchchallenge}[Stretch path: draw a frame]
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.5\linewidth}\RaggedRight
Add a sprite such as the Star. Drag it near a corner of the stage.\par\smallskip
Add the green \menu{Pen} blocks with \menu{Add Extension}.\par\smallskip
It stamps a star at each corner of a square!
\end{minipage}\hfill
