\kctitle{My Game Book}{One chapter per week: pictures, things to try and new words}

\begin{grownup}[How to use this book]
Read each chapter with your child; by now many children can read parts of it themselves. Point at
the pictures and let your child point too. Drawing, circling and saying the answer out loud all
count. Keep the book next to the computer during sessions.
\end{grownup}
