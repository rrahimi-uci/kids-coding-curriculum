# Exercise 3.1 – Pop the Balloon

**Goal:** count balloon pops with a score variable.
**Tool:** Scratch app. **Work with a partner:** Driver and Helper.

## Build steps

1. File > New. Click **Choose a Sprite**. Pick **Balloon1**.
2. Click **Variables** (orange-red). Click **Make a Variable**. Type `score`. Pick **For all sprites**. Click OK.
3. Click **Events** (yellow). Drag in **when green flag clicked**.
4. Snap on **set score to 0** and **show variable score** (orange-red Variables).
5. Drag in **when this sprite clicked** (yellow Events).
6. Snap on **change score by 1** (orange-red Variables).
7. Snap on **start sound Pop** (pink Sound) and **next costume** (purple Looks).
8. Click the flag. Click the balloon five times. Then click the flag again.

## The script in words

- when green flag clicked
- set score to 0
- show variable score

- when this sprite clicked
- change score by 1
- start sound Pop
- next costume

## Expected behavior

The score window on the stage starts at 0. Each click on the balloon pops,
changes its color, and adds 1 to the score. Click the flag: the score goes back to 0.

## Checklist

- [ ] My variable is called score, for all sprites.
- [ ] The score starts at 0 when I click the flag.
- [ ] Each pop adds 1.
