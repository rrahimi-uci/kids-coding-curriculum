## Hint 1: Where to look
Variable blocks are in the orange-red **Variables** circle. The **Make a Variable** button is at the top of that list.

## Hint 2: Try this
"set" puts a number in the box. "change" adds to the number already there. Check the little menu in each block says score. If you cannot see the score on the stage, click the checkbox next to score in the list.

## Hint 3: Almost there
Green flag script: "set score to ____", then "show variable score". Click script: "change score by ____", then the pop sound and next costume.
