\kctitle{Lesson Plans}{One plan per weekly 60-minute session, plus two optional buffer sessions}

\begin{tip}[How to use these plans]
Read the plan once (about 10 minutes) the day before. Every session has the same five parts:
\textbf{Warm-up, Show me, Make it, Wiggle break, Share}. Words in the \enquote{Teacher script}
boxes can be read aloud. Children aged 7--9 focus for about 10--15 minutes at a time, so the plan
changes activity often: follow the timer, not the page. The level runs 10 weeks; buffer
sessions B1 and B2 are optional (see the Teacher Guide's pacing section).
\end{tip}
