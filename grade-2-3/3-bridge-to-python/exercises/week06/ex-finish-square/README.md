# Exercise 6.2 - Finish the Square

**Goal:** type one new line to finish a square.
**Tool:** Thonny. **Work with a partner:** Driver and Helper.

## Steps

1. In Thonny, click **Load**. Open `starter.py` in the `ex-finish-square` folder.
2. Click **Run**. Count the sides. There are only 3!
3. Find the line that says `CHANGE ME`. Click on the empty line under it.
4. The Helper reads out one letter at a time. The Driver types: `t.forward(100)`
5. Look at your line and the line for side 3. Are they the same?
6. Click **Save**, then **Run**.

## What you should see

A black square with 4 sides that are all the same length. The turtle
ends in the corner where it started.

Red text? That is a typo. Look at the line number in the red text, and
check each letter of that line.

## Checklist

- [ ] I typed one new line.
- [ ] The square has 4 sides.
- [ ] The turtle ended where it started.

**Extra:** type `t.left(90)` under your new line. Now the turtle looks the same way as at the start.
