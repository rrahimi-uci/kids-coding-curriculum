## Hint 1: Where to look
Look for the pink-red My Blocks circle. After you make a block, a "define square" block appears in the code area. Is anything snapped under it?

## Hint 2: Try this
The "define square" block holds the steps for one square. The flag script uses the "square" block, then turns a little, again and again. Read the turn number from the turn card: a hexagon has 6 sides.

## Hint 3: Almost there
Flag script: repeat ____ with "square" and turn ____ inside. Under "define square": repeat 4 with move 60 and turn 90.
