## Hint 1: Where to look
Look at the two orange repeat blocks. One of them must sit inside the mouth of the other one, like a small doll inside a big doll.

## Hint 2: Try this
The inside repeat draws one square: move, then a quarter turn, 4 times. The outside repeat runs that square 4 times. Watch for the gray shadow when you drop a block: it shows where the block will go.
