# Exercise 9.2 – Build My Big Build

**Goal:** build your Big Build one part at a time.
**Tool:** Scratch app (Path A) or Thonny (Path B). **Work with a partner:** Driver and Helper.

## Build steps

1. Open the project your grown-up got ready.
2. Read Part 1 on your plan card. Build only Part 1.
3. **Test it:** click the green flag, or press the green **Run** button. Check it off.
4. Do the same for Part 2 and Part 3.
5. **Python:** change one `CHANGE ME` line at a time. Keep the quote marks around color names.
6. Save often!

## The script in words

**Path A:** Leo's Flower Garden.

- when green flag clicked (yellow Events): erase all (green Pen), set flowers to 0 (orange-red Variables)
- when space key pressed: go to mouse-pointer (blue Motion), pen down
- repeat 6 (orange Control)
  - repeat 4: move 30 steps, turn right 90 degrees
  - turn right 60 degrees
- pen up, change flowers by 1

**Path B:** Omar's postcard: a blue house with sides of 120.

## Expected behavior

Path A: space draws a flower, and the counter goes up by 1.
Path B: a house and roof in two colors you chose.
