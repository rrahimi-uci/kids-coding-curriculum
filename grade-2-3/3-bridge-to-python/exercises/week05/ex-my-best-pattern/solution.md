# Teacher reference: Exercise 5.3 - My Best Pattern
> TEACHER ONLY. Keep this file away from students. It is not published on the website.

**Level and week:** Junior Level 3, Week 5 (Play Day: Blocks to Words)  **Kind:** Scratch (offline app), planned build and free play  **Time:** about 12 minutes
**Big idea:** Plan a program in parts, then build and test one part at a time; a repeat inside a repeat turns one shape into a pattern.

## The finished project
Setup: File > New, add the Pen extension, drag the cat to the middle of the stage. The workbook's sample pattern; every child's pattern will differ.

Sprite1 (the cat)
- when green flag clicked
- erase all
- set pen color to purple
- pen down
- repeat 6
  - repeat 4
    - move 50 steps
    - turn right 90 degrees
  - turn right 60 degrees
- say Ta-da! for 2 seconds

Plan (written first): Part 1 get ready (erase, purple pen, pen down); Part 2 the pattern (six squares in a ring); Part 3 the ending (say Ta-da!).

## What you should see
- Six purple squares around one point, each turned a little from the last, like a flower; then "Ta-da!" for 2 seconds.
- Other good patterns: repeat 4 with a quarter turn (the window from week 1); triangles (inside repeat 3, turn 120) in a ring; a "square" My Block used inside the outside repeat; a pen size change for thicker lines.

## Talking points
- Ask: "What does the inside repeat draw? What does the outside repeat do?" (One shape; it draws the shape again and turns.)
- Planning in parts (week 2) makes testing easier: a bug in Part 2 does not hide in Part 1.
- If the idea came from a friend's pattern, say so at the Share.

## Common mistakes and quick fixes
- The outside turn is inside the inside repeat: the shape breaks (relay leg 1). Move it under the inside repeat.
- No pen down, so nothing is drawn: add it in Part 1.
- Old drawings pile up: "erase all" at the top of Part 1.
- The pattern runs off the stage: drag the cat to the middle, or make the move number smaller.

## Check-off list
- [ ] Three parts are written or drawn in the workbook before building.
- [ ] A repeat sits inside another repeat.
- [ ] The pattern draws when the green flag is clicked.
- [ ] The child explains what the inside and outside repeats do (show-me rows 1 and 2).
