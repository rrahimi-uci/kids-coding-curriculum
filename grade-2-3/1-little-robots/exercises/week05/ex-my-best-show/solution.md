# Teacher reference: Exercise 5.3 - My Best Show
> TEACHER ONLY. Keep this file away from students. It is not published on the website.

**Level and week:** Junior Level 1, Week 5 (Play Day and Show Me)  **Kind:** Scratch (offline app), free build  **Time:** about 8 minutes
**Big idea:** A show is a green-flag script; a repeat does steps again so we do not have to snap them on many times.

## The finished project
Setup: File > New. Keep the cat. This is one example; any show with a green flag and a repeat is right.

Sprite: Sprite1 (the cat)
- when green flag clicked
- say Watch me! for 2 seconds
- repeat 4
  - next costume
  - move 10 steps
  - wait 1 seconds
- say Ta-da! for 2 seconds

## What you should see
- A speech bubble "Watch me!" for 2 seconds.
- The cat takes four small walking steps to the right, changing costume each time, with a 1-second pause between steps.
- A speech bubble "Ta-da!" for 2 seconds.

## Talking points
- Ask "Tell me what your script does." A good answer follows the script top to bottom: "It says watch me, walks four times, then says ta-da."
- Ask "How many times does it walk? How do you know?" (The repeat says 4.)
- Ask "What would change if the repeat said 8?" Let them predict, then test.
- This is the moment to check off the show-me rows for "snaps a green-flag script", "uses repeat" and "says what their script does".

## Common mistakes and quick fixes
- A block snaps under the repeat instead of inside it: it runs once, after the loop. Drag it into the mouth of the repeat.
- No wait inside the repeat: the walk is too fast to see. Add wait 1 seconds (orange Control) inside.
- The cat walks off the stage after many clicks: drag it back with the mouse.
- A real name in a speech bubble: change it to a nickname.

## Check-off list
- [ ] The script starts with "when green flag clicked".
- [ ] It has a repeat with at least one block inside the mouth.
- [ ] All blocks are snapped together: dragging the top block moves the whole script.
- [ ] The child says, in their own words, what the script does.
