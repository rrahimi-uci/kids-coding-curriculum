# Exercise 5.3 – My Best Show

**Goal:** make your own show with the green flag and a repeat.
**Tool:** Scratch app. **Work with a partner:** Driver and Helper, swap on the timer.

## Build steps

1. Click **Events** (yellow). Drag in **when green flag clicked**.
2. Click **Looks** (purple). Snap on **say Hello! for 2 seconds**. Type `Watch me!`.
3. Click **Control** (orange). Snap on **repeat 10**. Change 10 to `4`.
4. Put **next costume** (purple Looks) inside the repeat.
5. Put **move 10 steps** (blue Motion) inside too.
6. Put **wait 1 seconds** (orange Control) inside too.
7. Under the repeat, snap on **say Ta-da! for 2 seconds** (purple Looks).
8. Click the green flag. Then make it yours!

## The script in words

- when green flag clicked
- say Watch me! for 2 seconds
- repeat 4
  - next costume
  - move 10 steps
  - wait 1 seconds
- say Ta-da! for 2 seconds

## Expected behavior

The cat says "Watch me!". It walks four little steps, slowly. Then it says "Ta-da!".
Your own show can be different. It needs the green flag and a repeat.
