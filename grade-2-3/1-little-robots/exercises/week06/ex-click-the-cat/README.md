# Exercise 6.1 – Click the Cat

**Goal:** make the cat meow and talk when you click it.
**Tool:** Scratch app. **Work with a partner:** Driver and Helper.

## Build steps

1. Click **Events** (yellow). Drag in **when this sprite clicked**.
2. Click **Sound** (pink). Snap on **play sound Meow until done**.
3. Click **Looks** (purple). Snap on **say Hello! for 2 seconds**. Type `That tickles!`.
4. Do not click the green flag. Click the cat on the stage!

Click quickly. If you hold the mouse down and move, you drag the cat instead.

## The script in words

- when this sprite clicked
- play sound Meow until done
- say That tickles! for 2 seconds

## Expected behavior

Nothing happens until you click the cat. Then the cat meows and says
"That tickles!". It works every time you click the cat.

## Checklist

- [ ] My script starts with the "when this sprite clicked" block.
- [ ] All my blocks are snapped together.
- [ ] When I click the cat, it meows and talks.
