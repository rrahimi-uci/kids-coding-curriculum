# Exercise 8.3 – Stamp a Ring

**Goal:** stamp a ring of six cats.
**Tool:** Scratch app. **Work with a partner:** Driver and Helper.

## Build steps

1. No green **Pen** circle? Click **Add Extension**. Choose **Pen**.
2. Click **Events** (yellow). Drag in **when green flag clicked**.
3. Click **Looks** (purple). Snap on **set size to 100 %**. Change 100 to `40`.
4. Click **Pen** (green). Snap on **erase all**.
5. Click **Control** (orange). Snap on **repeat 10**. Change 10 to `6`.
6. Click **Pen** (green). Put **stamp** inside the repeat.
7. Click **Motion** (blue). Put **move 10 steps** under stamp. Change 10 to `60`.
8. Put **turn ↻ 60 degrees** under the move. Read the turn card: six sides is `60`.
9. Click the green flag.

## The script in words

- when green flag clicked
- set size to 40 %
- erase all
- repeat 6: stamp, move 60 steps, turn ↻ 60 degrees

## Expected behavior

The cat leaves six pictures of itself in a ring, like a flower.
Each cat is turned a little more than the one before.

## Checklist

- [ ] Stamp, move and turn are all inside the repeat.
- [ ] I used the turn card for the turn.
- [ ] I see six cats in a ring.
