# Teacher reference: Exercise 8.3 - Stamp a Ring
> TEACHER ONLY. Keep this file away from students. It is not published on the website.

**Level and week:** Junior Level 1, Week 8 (Draw With the Pen)  **Kind:** Scratch (offline app)  **Time:** about 6 minutes
**Big idea:** Stamp leaves a picture of the sprite; inside a repeat with a move and a turn it makes a pattern.

## The finished project
Setup: the Pen extension is added (Add Extension > Pen). Keep the cat.

Sprite: Sprite1 (the cat)
- when green flag clicked
- set size to 40 %
- erase all
- repeat 6
  - stamp
  - move 60 steps
  - turn ↻ 60 degrees

## What you should see
- After the green flag: six cats in a ring (the corners of a hexagon), each turned a little more than the one before, like petals. The real cat ends on top of the first stamp.
- Clicking the flag again draws the same ring: "erase all" clears the old stamps first.

## Talking points
- The 60 comes from the turn card (hexagon, 6 sides). We read turns off the card; children do not compute them.
- Stamp is like a rubber stamp: the sprite leaves a copy and keeps moving.
- Changing the number in "move" makes the ring bigger or smaller. Changing the costume between stamps (Looks, "next costume") makes a walking ring.

## Common mistakes and quick fixes
- Only one cat: stamp is outside the repeat, or the repeat holds only stamp. Put stamp, move and turn all inside.
- Cats in a straight line: the turn is missing or outside the repeat.
- The ring is cut off at the edge: drag the cat nearer the middle before clicking the flag.
- A ring of 4 or 5 cats with a gap: the repeat number does not match the turn card (6 and 60 go together).

## Check-off list
- [ ] Stamp, move and turn are inside "repeat 6": dragging the repeat moves all three.
- [ ] The turn is 60, from the turn card: the child points to the hexagon line on the card.
- [ ] Six cats show in a ring after the green flag: watch one run.
