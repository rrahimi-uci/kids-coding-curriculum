# Exercise 9.2 – Build My Card

**Goal:** build your card, one storyboard box at a time.
**Tool:** Scratch app. **Work with a partner:** Driver and Helper.

## Build steps

1. Open your card. The backdrop and first sprite are ready.
2. **Box 1, start.** Click **Events** (yellow). Drag in **when green flag clicked**. Click **Looks** (purple). Snap on a **say** block. Add a sound from **Sound** (pink).
3. Click the green flag to test. Check off box 1.
4. **Box 2, middle.** Click **Control** (orange). Snap on **repeat**. Put **next costume** (Looks, purple) and **wait 1 seconds** inside. Test it.
5. **Box 3, end.** Add a second sprite and click it. From **Events** (yellow), drag in **when this sprite clicked**. Add a sound or a say block. Test it.
6. Save: **File**, then **Save to your computer**.

## The script in words

Mei's card (yours can differ):

- Cat: when green flag clicked, say Happy birthday!, play sound Meow, repeat 4: next costume, wait 1 seconds
- Balloon1: when this sprite clicked, play sound Pop, say Surprise!

## Expected behavior

The green flag starts box 1 and box 2. A click starts box 3.

## Checklist

- [ ] The green flag starts my card.
- [ ] Something repeats.
- [ ] A click or a key does something.
- [ ] I hear a sound.
- [ ] I have two sprites, or the backdrop changes.
