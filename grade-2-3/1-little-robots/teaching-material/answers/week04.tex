\answersweek{4}{Repeat It!}{Answers for workbook chapter 4 and play-at-home card 4.}

\section{Lesson questions}
\begin{answer}[Exit question]
3 meows. To hear 5 meows, change the number in the repeat block from 3 to 5.
\end{answer}

\begin{answer}[Reflection]
\enquote{What does a repeat block do?} It runs the blocks inside it again, as many times as the number says (or \enquote{it does it again and again}, \enquote{so we need fewer blocks}). \enquote{How did we make sure everyone had a fair turn?} We swapped when the timer ran out. Any drawing of a dance move is right.
\end{answer}

\section{Workbook exercises}
\begin{answer}[Picture: the long way and the repeat way]
The repeat way is shorter: 3 blocks instead of 9. Both scripts make the sprite change costume 4 times, one second apart.
\end{answer}

\begin{answer}[Exercise 4.1: Dance, Ballerina!]
\textbf{What you should see:} after the green flag, the ballerina changes pose 4 times, once every second, and ends in the pose she started in. Counting out loud gives four moves.

\textbf{What to look for:} \tickbox{} the script is on the ballerina, not the cat \quad \tickbox{} \enquote{next costume} and \enquote{wait 1 seconds} sit inside the C \quad \tickbox{} the number is 4.
\end{answer}

\begin{answer}[Exercise 4.2: The Noisy Cat]
\textbf{With the bug:} the cat meows 10 times (about 10 seconds). That is too many. \textbf{After the fix:} the number is 3 and the cat meows 3 times, then stops. The script must be on the cat (the cat is selected in the sprite list).
\end{answer}

\begin{answer}[Exercise 4.3: Too Many or Too Few?]
Too few. With \enquote{repeat 2} the ballerina does only 2 of her 4 moves and stops in a different pose. The number should be 4.
\end{answer}

\section{Play-at-home card}
\begin{answer}[Dance loops at home]
Look for two cards inside the loop and a number on the REPEAT card. The dance is right if everyone does the inside cards that many times. For the bug, the child should say \enquote{too many} when the grown-up does it more times than the number, and \enquote{too few} when fewer.
\end{answer}

\begin{answer}[Repeat hunt]
Any real repeat counts: a chorus in a song, steps on the stairs, brushing teeth, the wheels on the bus going round and round. A number for how many times is a bonus.
\end{answer}
