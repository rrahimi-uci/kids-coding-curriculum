\kctitle{My Robot Book}{One chapter per week: pictures, things to try and new words}

\begin{grownup}[How to use this book]
Read each chapter aloud with your child. Point at the pictures and let your child point too.
Your child does not need to read or write much: drawing, circling and saying the answer out
loud all count. Keep the book next to the computer during sessions.
\end{grownup}
