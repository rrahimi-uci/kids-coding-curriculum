\kctitle{My Progress Tracker}{One copy per child: what I can do, week by week}

\begin{grownup}[How the tracker works]
At the end of each session (2 minutes, in the Share part), read the week's \enquote{I can} line
aloud. When your child feels ready, they color the star. A new idea can wait: the same ideas come
back in later weeks, so a star can be colored any time. Keep the tracker in a folder and bring it
out at the showcase.
\end{grownup}
