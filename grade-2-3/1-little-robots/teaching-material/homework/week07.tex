\homeworkweek{7}{Two Sprites Talk}{Optional. One short game with a grown-up.}

\begin{playathome}[Knock, knock!]
Tell a knock-knock joke with a grown-up. Take turns. Wait for the other person before you talk.
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=2pt]
  \item You: \enquote{Knock, knock!}
  \item Grown-up: \enquote{Who is there?}
  \item You: \enquote{Boo.}
  \item Grown-up: \enquote{Boo who?}
  \item You: \enquote{Do not cry, it is only a joke!}
\end{enumerate}
Now try it with no waiting: both talk at once. Is it still funny?
\end{playathome}

\begin{playathome}[Credit detective]
Pick a picture book. Find who wrote the words and who drew the pictures. Their names are on the cover. That is giving credit!
\end{playathome}

\begin{selfcheck}
  \item We took turns to talk.
  \item I found who made a book.
  \item I can say who made the Scratch sprites.
\end{selfcheck}
