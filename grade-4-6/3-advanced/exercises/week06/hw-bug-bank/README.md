# Homework 6 - Piggy Bank Bug Hunt

**Goal:** find and fix the 3 bugs in the `PiggyBank` class. When it works,
Run prints:

```
Comet has 0 coins
1. in 10
2. in 5
3. out 15
```

**How to hunt:** press **Run** and compare with the expected output. Then
use **Debug** and **Step over** to watch `self.coins` and `self.log` in
the **Variables** view. Write each bug you find on your homework sheet
(line number, what was wrong, how you fixed it).

**Check your work:** open `check_my_work.py` and press **Run**.
