\begin{core}[Pixel Pet bug hunt (Thonny, 15 minutes)]
Open \texttt{exercises/week06/hw-bug-pixel-pet/starter.py}. It is a virtual pet stored in a dictionary, and it has \textbf{three} bugs.
The rules: \texttt{feed()} takes 3 from hunger (never below 0); \texttt{play()} adds 2 to happiness and 1 to hunger;
\texttt{mood()} says \texttt{"grumpy"} if hunger is 8 or more \textbf{or} happiness is 2 or less, otherwise \texttt{"happy"}.
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.4em]
  \item Press \menu{Run}. Read the traceback bottom-up and fix bug 1.
  \item Run again and look at the printed dictionary. Is there a key that should not be there? That is bug 2.
  \item Bug 3 does not crash. Look at the last line: Biscuit has hunger 9. What do the rules say Biscuit should feel?
        Explain \texttt{mood()} out loud (rubber duck!) and compare it with the rules.
  \item Done looks like: \texttt{Pixel feels happy.} and \texttt{Biscuit feels grumpy.} (The whole output is in the folder's \texttt{README.md}.)
  \item Open \texttt{check\_my\_work.py} and press \menu{Run}.
\end{enumerate}
