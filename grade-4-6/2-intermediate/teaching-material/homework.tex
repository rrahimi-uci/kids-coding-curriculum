\kctitle{Homework Sets}{15--20 minutes a week: Core, Challenge and Creative}

\begin{tip}[How homework works]
Do the \textbf{Core} task first (about 10 minutes). \textbf{Challenge} and \textbf{Creative} are
optional extras for when you want more. Finish with the self-check list. If you get stuck for more than
5 minutes, write down what you tried and bring the question next week: that is a great result too.
\end{tip}
