# Exercise 1.1 – Hello, Cat!

**Goal:** make the cat say hello, walk, meow and say bye, in that order.
**Tool:** Scratch app (offline). **Work in pairs:** driver clicks, navigator reads these steps. Swap when the timer rings.

## Build steps

1. Open the Scratch app. You will see the cat on the stage.
2. Click **Events** (yellow circle). Drag **when green flag clicked** into the code area.
3. Click **Motion**. Snap **go to x: (0) y: (0)** under it. (0, 0 is the middle of the stage.)
4. Click **Looks**. Snap **say (Hello!) for (2) seconds**. Click the text and type `Hi! I am` plus your **nickname**.
5. Click **Motion**. Snap **move (10) steps** and change 10 to `100`.
6. Click **Sound**. Snap **play sound (Meow) until done**.
7. Click **Looks**. Snap another **say (...) for (2) seconds** and type `Bye!`.
8. Click the green flag to test.
9. Save: **File > Save to your computer**, folder `Scratch Projects`, name `hello-cat`.

## The script in words

Cat sprite, one script:

- when green flag clicked
- go to x: 0 y: 0
- say "Hi! I am Pixel Panda" for 2 seconds (use your own nickname)
- move 100 steps
- play sound Meow until done
- say "Bye!" for 2 seconds

## Expected behavior

When you click the green flag, the cat jumps to the middle, says hello with
your nickname, walks to the right, meows, and says "Bye!". Clicking the flag
again plays the same show from the middle.
