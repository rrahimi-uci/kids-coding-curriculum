## Hint 1: Where to look
Click the green flag and watch the cat closely. Does it jump up first, or go down first? Say out loud what you expected and what really happened.

## Hint 2: Try this
Read your blocks one at a time with your finger and act each one out. The **change y by** blocks in the **Motion** category move the cat up or down. A positive number means up. A negative number means down.

## Hint 3: Almost there
Think about the jump in order: up first, then back down. Look at the numbers in your blocks and ask which way each one moves the cat. Try dragging one block to a different spot, then click the flag. Change only one thing at a time.
