## Hint 1: Where to look
Find the three `# TODO` comments in `starter.py`. Each one tells you what to print. Look at the line `print("Hello, Python!")` that already works, because your new lines will look a lot like it.

## Hint 2: Try this
The `print` command shows words in the Shell. The words go inside quotes, and the quotes go inside the parentheses. Write one new `print` line under each `# TODO`, and press Run after each one.

## Hint 3: Almost there
Each new line has this shape: `print("____")`. Put your own words in the blank. Check that every `(` has a `)` and every opening quote has a closing quote. Your last message needs the word `bye` somewhere in it.
