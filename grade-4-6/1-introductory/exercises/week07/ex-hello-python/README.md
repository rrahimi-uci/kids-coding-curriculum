# Exercise 7.1 - Hello, Python!

**Goal:** make Python say four things in the Shell.

1. Open `starter.py` in Thonny (**File > Open...**).
2. Press the green **Run** button (or **F5**). Look at the Shell at the
   bottom: Python says `Hello, Python!`.
3. Follow the `# TODO` comments. Add three more `print(...)` lines:
   * who is coding today (a nickname is fine - never your full name),
   * your favorite game or animal,
   * a goodbye message that uses the word `bye`.
4. Press **Run** again after every new line.

**Remember:** Python is picky. `print` is all small letters, the words go
inside "quotes", and every `(` needs a `)`.

**Done looks like:** four different lines in the Shell, the last one says bye.

**Check your work:** open `check_my_work.py` and press **Run**.

**Extra:** add a comment line (starting with `#`) that explains your program,
or print a tiny picture made of letters, like `print("(^_^)")`.
