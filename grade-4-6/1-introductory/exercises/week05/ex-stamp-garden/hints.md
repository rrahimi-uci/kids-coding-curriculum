## Hint 1: Where to look
Your green-flag script should read like a plan: grass first, then flowers. The details live inside the two pink blocks in the My Blocks category, `grass` and `flower`.

## Hint 2: Try this
Flowers are copies of your painted sprite. Use the **stamp** block from the Pen category. To make every flower different, use **pick random** blocks (Operators category) for the position and the size. **change color effect** (Looks category) changes the color.
