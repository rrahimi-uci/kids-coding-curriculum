\kctitle{Lesson Plans}{One plan per weekly 60-minute session, plus two optional buffer sessions}

\begin{tip}[How to use these plans]
Read the plan once (about 10 minutes) the day before. Every session has the same five parts:
\textbf{Warm-up, Concept, Guided demo, Hands-on build, Wrap-up}. Words in the
\enquote{Teacher script} boxes can be read aloud. The level runs 10--12 weeks (see the
Teacher Guide's pacing section). Buffer sessions B1 and B2 are optional: use them only when there is
room inside the 12 weeks, for example before a holiday or when the group needs a change of pace.
\end{tip}
